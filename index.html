FAQ

QUESTIONS ANSWERED

Who are these programs for?
Any hockey player serious about improving their off-ice performance — from competitive youth players to adult beer leaguers and juniors. All programs are structured with progressions so beginners and advanced players both benefit.
Do I need a gym membership?
The free 5-day program is fully bodyweight. The 12-week program uses basic gym equipment (barbell, dumbbells, cable). The Coach's pack includes both gym and bodyweight options for flexibility.
Is there a refund policy?
Yes — 30-day money-back guarantee on all paid programs. If you're not happy with it, just email and we'll sort it. No questions, no hassle.
What format do I receive the programs in?
PDF with embedded video demonstration links. Download once, use on any device. The PDF is printable and includes exercise photos, rep/set schemes, and coaching notes for each session.
What is the Elite Hockey Drills App?
An off-ice training app built specifically for hockey players — 228+ exercises across 15 categories, full structured programs, and progress tracking. Launching soon. Join the waitlist to get founding member pricing when it drops.
Do you offer 1-on-1 coaching?
Not currently — the programs and the upcoming app are the focus. They're built to deliver the same systematic approach Coach David uses with national-level athletes, just in a scalable format you can run independently.
