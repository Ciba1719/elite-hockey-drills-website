The off-ice years from 9 to 11 are the most important athletic development window in your child's life — and most kids waste them.

This is the program that doesn't.

Twelve weeks. Three sessions a week. Forty-five to sixty minutes each. Bodyweight and bands only — no gym, no equipment, no excuses. Built around the international Long-Term Athletic Development model used by national hockey federations worldwide, designed for the window where coordination, balance, speed mechanics, and movement quality lock in for life.

Inside the program

What's included.

  • 01
    3 progressive blocks

    Movement Fundamentals → Coordination & Strength → Athleticism & Play. Each block builds on the one before it.

  • 02
    36 complete sessions

    Every exercise specified: sets, reps, rest, coaching cues. Zero guesswork.

  • 03
    Age-appropriate testing battery

    Track real progress every 4 weeks with movement screens designed for the 9-11 window.

  • 04
    Full RAMP warm-up & cool-down protocol

    Built into every single session. Injury prevention starts here.

  • 05
    Adductor & prehab work built in

    Building the durability that prevents the groin and knee issues that derail older players.

  • 06
    Mindset, sleep, & nutrition basics

    Written for kids and parents to read together. Hockey performance starts off the rink.

  • 07
    Exercise glossary with coaching cues

    So any parent can coach the session — even with no athletic background.

This is not "youth fitness." This is the same coaching framework used with elite youth national-team athletes — scaled, paced, and written for the 9 to 11 window.

Your child will move better, feel more confident, and arrive at tryouts looking like a kid who's been training all summer. Because they have.
